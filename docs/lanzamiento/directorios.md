## Datos de la ficha

Nombre: DoorManager Pro

Autor: Francisco Javier Ena Márquez

Website: https://demo-doormanager-pro.pages.dev/

Tagline: Connect field work, inspections and the office

Short description:

DoorManager Pro connects customers, equipment, work orders, inspections and
business operations for installation and maintenance teams. Explore a guided
public beta with fictional data, without signing up. The interface is currently
in Spanish.

Long description:

Installation and maintenance work does not end when the technician leaves the
site. The office still needs a clear record of what happened, what was used and
what can be invoiced.

DoorManager Pro brings those steps into one connected workflow: customer and
equipment records, work planning, component-level inspections, technician reports,
invoicing examples and cost analysis.

The public beta offers a small, guided selection of the application. Choose a
business role, explore prepared examples or create a fictional workflow. Each
visitor uses a temporary demo workspace. Please use fictional data only.

The interface and tutorial videos are currently in Spanish. I am looking for
feedback from installation, maintenance and field-service teams, especially where
the workflow feels unclear or does not match the way they actually work.

## Maker comment

Hi! I'm Francisco Javier, the creator of DoorManager Pro.

I am building DMP around a simple problem: information often gets lost between a
customer request, the technician's visit and the office that needs to follow up.

This beta lets you try part of that connected workflow with fictional data and a
step-by-step guide. It is an early product, and I would value practical feedback
from installation and maintenance teams.

Which handoff in your workflow is still the most difficult to keep organized?
